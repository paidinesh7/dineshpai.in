Business & investments @ Zerodha

I grew up by the River Seetha in Udupi. After studying computer science at RVCE (2014), I started my career in financial research at D.E. Shaw India and later completed my MBA at the University of Calgary.

Along the way, I built and shut down two small businesses—a textbook delivery service in Bangalore and a student housing venture in Canada. Dealing firsthand with working capital cycles, vendor terms, and thin operating margins taught me more about business mortality than any financial model.

Since 2020, I have been at Zerodha, where I lead private market investments at Rainmatter and work on long-term ecological and rural livelihood initiatives through the Rainmatter Foundation.
