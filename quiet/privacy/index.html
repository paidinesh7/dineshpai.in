LEGAL • SOVEREIGN PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for Quiet

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Open Source (MIT)

The Sovereign Guarantee: Quiet is built on an anti-engagement, local-first philosophy. We collect zero personal data, track zero analytics, display zero advertisements, and operate zero remote databases. All calculations and documents remain exclusively on your device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Quiet does not require user registration, account creation, email addresses, or phone numbers. When you use Quiet:

  • On-Device Storage: All financial entries (liquid buffers, expenses, recurring leaks, tax checklist statuses, and notes) are stored locally in your device's native sandboxed storage (AsyncStorage).
  • Zero Data Exfiltration: No financial data, transaction numbers, or personal identifying information is ever transmitted to any external server or third-party cloud.
  • Local Statement Parsing: If you scan or paste bank statement text, all regex matching and categorization is processed in volatile device memory (RAM) and immediately discarded.

2. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with the Google Play Developer Policy and Data Safety requirements:

  • Data Collected: None. Quiet collects 0 bytes of user data.
  • Data Shared: None. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third parties or advertisers.
  • Third-Party SDKs: Quiet contains zero tracking libraries, zero social media SDKs, and zero behavioral advertising frameworks.

3. Device Permissions & Biometrics

  • Biometric Authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock): Quiet offers an optional native app lock. Authentication is performed directly by your device's secure hardware enclave (Android BiometricPrompt). Quiet never accesses, reads, or stores your biometric data.
  • Storage & File Access: Used solely to import statements or export your encrypted CA dossier locally on your device upon your explicit request.

4. Security & Data Retention

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain complete sovereignty over your information:

  • You may reset your data at any time via the About > Reset Data button inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored data from your device's sandbox.

5. Open Source Transparency

Quiet is licensed under the permissive MIT License. The complete source code is public and open for independent security auditing to verify our zero-telemetry claims.
