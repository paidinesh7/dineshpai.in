Three Things Retail Investors Should Know

What the Jio DRHP means for existing Reliance shareholders and IPO applicants
100% Fresh Issue
No early investors are exiting

Unlike most mega-IPOs where PE funds cash out via an Offer for Sale, Meta, Google, and PE funds are selling zero shares. Every rupee raised goes to Jio to pay down debt.

RIL Quota
Reserved quota, not free shares

Because this is an IPO and not a demerger (unlike Jio Financial in 2023), RIL shareholders won't get free shares—but the DRHP reserves a special RIL Shareholder Quota to bid.

2.9% Dilution
Why SEBI's 2.5% rule mattered

SEBI's new mega-IPO rule (cutting minimum float from 5% to 2.5% for >₹5L Cr companies) lets Jio list by issuing just 27 Cr fresh shares (~2.9% stake) without flooding the market.
