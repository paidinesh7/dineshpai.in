Three Rules Every Investor Should Know

How exemptions, transaction taxes, and losses work on your investments
₹1.25 Lakh
Tax-free profit every year

Your first ₹1.25 Lakh of long-term profit in stocks and equity mutual funds each financial year is completely tax-free.

Paid on Loss
STT is not deductible

STT (0.1% on buy + 0.1% on sell) is charged on the full trade value even when you sell at a loss, and cannot be deducted from your taxable profit.

8 Years
Carry forward your losses

Short-term losses reduce both short-term and long-term gains. Unused losses can be carried forward for 8 years if you file your ITR on time.

Source: Income Tax Act — Sections 112A, 48 & 74 07 / 08

Some Suggestions

Three ways to make stock market taxation fairer for long-term savers
01
Credit STT against LTCG

Let investors deduct the STT already paid on their trades from their long-term capital gains tax bill.

02
0% tax after 5 years

Reward patient capital by making equity holdings held for more than 5 years completely tax-free.

03
Adjust gains for inflation

Bring back indexation so investors pay tax only on real gains above the Cost Inflation Index.
