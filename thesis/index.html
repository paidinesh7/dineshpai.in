Investment Memorandum · Ten-Year Horizon 2026 — 2036

Investing in what becomes scarce, and the physical stack India must own.

Over the next decade, tens of millions of Indian households will cross into meaningful disposable income, while geopolitical friction forces India to localize critical industrial infrastructure. Digital distribution in India is now abundant. The durable returns over the next ten years will flow to the hard-to-scale supply bottlenecks underneath those two curves.

01 / The Premise Abundance vs. Bottlenecks

Most venture capital in India still chases the abundant layer of the economy—consumer apps, marketing-led D2C brands, and thin software wrappers where marginal supply is infinite.

When an economy compounds and software becomes easier to build with AI, value migrates away from digital interfaces and toward inputs that cannot be replicated overnight: physical capacity, specialized hardware, scarce IP, and metallurgical or industrial know-how.

My 10-year outlook rests on two convictions: first, that rising middle-class disposable income will create acute scarcity in physical experiences, housing, healthcare, and hardware; and second, that successive Indian governments will have no choice but to back domestic resilience in grid infrastructure, industrial machinery, and critical metals.

02 / Income Mobilization & Scarcity The $3,000 → $5,000+ Inflection

When households cross the $3,000 to $5,000 per-capita income threshold, basic subsistence is already paid for. Incremental income flows disproportionately into housing quality, travel, live entertainment, preventive health, and financial assets.

While India's national per-capita GDP sits near $2,900, our top eight metropolitan corridors and over 60 million consuming households are already operating in the $5,000–$12,000+ band. We do not have to guess what happens next; we saw the exact same curve play out in Japan in the 1970s, South Korea in the late 1980s, and China between 2008 and 2018.

  • Housing & Managed Communities: As nuclear families upgrade from basic shelter to well-designed, energy- and water-resilient communities, developable urban land and high-trust real estate execution remain severely supply-constrained.
  • Experiential Travel & Mid-Market Hospitality: Domestic air and highway connectivity have expanded faster than quality, standardized mid-market stays and destination infrastructure outside Tier-1 metros.
  • Clean, Traceable Nutrition: Rising incomes in India have come alongside a severe metabolic health crisis. Families are shifting from unbranded staples to high-protein, chemical-free, and clean-label food—where the bottleneck is not marketing, but farm-level traceability and cold-chain integrity (Akshayakalpa, The Whole Truth, Two Brothers).
  • Unconflicted Household Finance: As household savings move from physical gold and fixed deposits into capital markets and credit, transparent, fee-only wealth and retirement infrastructure will compound steadily (Capitalmind, Assetplus, Mprofit).

04 / Sovereign Industrial Stack Grid, Machinery & Critical Metals

India cannot build a resilient industrial economy—or run AI data centers, EV fleets, and automated factories—while remaining import-dependent on the physical machinery, grid equipment, and refined metals underneath them.

Building resilience across these layers is a non-partisan priority that successive governments will continue to fund and protect over the next decade. Because these businesses require 4–6 years of metallurgical, electrical, or mechanical qualification, traditional software-focused funds overlook them—leaving massive operating leverage for patient capital.

  • The Grid Is the Real Clean-Energy Bottleneck: Adding solar panels is no longer hard; evacuating intermittent power, managing thermal runaway (Voltseal), and upgrading distribution transformers and industrial energy markets (Neufin) is where the margin pool is shifting.
  • Metallurgy Underpins Sovereignty: Look one layer below India's transformer shortage and you find that nearly 90% of Cold-Rolled Grain-Oriented (CRGO) electrical steel is imported from four countries. Similar choke points exist in rare-earth magnets and battery-grade refining. Engineering around imported materials—or refining them domestically—is a foundational 10-year theme.
  • "The Machines That Make the Machines": True manufacturing depth comes when Indian companies stop merely assembling imported sub-components on imported machines and start building the precision capital goods, sensors, and factory tooling domestically.
05 / Capital Horizon Structure Dictates Strategy

You cannot back 10-year physical bottlenecks with 3-year markup expectations.

Whether a founder is building a network of step-down rehabilitation hospitals, a new sports league, a non-rare-earth electric motor, or precision industrial machinery, the first four years look slow on a spreadsheet. Qualifying hardware, earning consumer trust, and building physical capacity take time before operating leverage kicks in.

At Rainmatter, investing from a permanent balance sheet with zero external LPs and no forced exit timelines allows us to underwrite these exact curves—backing founders who prioritize unit-economic durability and engineering depth over premature blitzscaling.
